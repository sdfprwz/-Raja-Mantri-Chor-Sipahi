📜 How to play
  1. 4 chits — Raja, Mantri, Chor, Sipahi — are dealt secretly each round.
  2. Your chit shows for 7 seconds then hides (anti-sneak 👀). Use Peek to glance again briefly.
  3. Sipahi must catch the Chor by picking a suspect.
  4. Correct guess → Sipahi 500, Chor 0. Wrong guess → swapped! Raja 1000, Mantri 800 fixed.
  5. Most points after all rounds wins 🏆. Short of 4 friends? Add bots 🤖.
  6. Host picks any 1–500 rounds, or ♾️ endless — and can extend/shorten mid-game. 🔊 Sounds alert you: round start, chit hide, Sipahi turn, countdown ticks, catch/miss & victory. Toggle with the speaker button up top.
