👑 Privacy Policy — Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi

Last updated: 14 September 2026

This app offers online multiplayer rooms for the traditional card game Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi.

Data we collect

  • Nickname you type to join a room (up to 15 chars).
  • Room chat messages — kept in memory only (last 50 per room), deleted when the server restarts.
  • Game state (scores, roles) — in-memory only, no database.
  • Technical logs from our host (e.g. Render) such as IP address, for security and debugging.

Data we do NOT collect

  • No accounts, no passwords, no email, no location, no contacts, no advertising IDs.

Multiplayer

Nicknames, scores and chat are shared with other players in the same room. Do not share personal info in chat.

Children

No age gate; parental guidance recommended for chat with strangers. Hosts can play with bots instead.

Contact / deletion

Rooms are ephemeral. To request removal of hosted log data, contact the publisher via the store listing support email.
